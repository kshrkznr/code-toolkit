# Resolver Reading Strategy

## Observation

Author's Recipe is highly effective as a concrete example.

Because it is concrete, however, both people and AI may treat it as the primary
interpretation of a concept rather than one possible practice.

The influence may come from several places:

- project instructions
- the Documentation Resolver
- document ordering
- search and reading paths

The issue may therefore be the path by which a reader reaches Author's Recipe,
rather than the responsibility or content of Author's Recipe itself.

## Hypothesis

A reader may interpret a concrete practice more appropriately after first
understanding the accepted responsibility that it illustrates.

This does not imply one fixed reading order. The relevant path depends on the
current question.

Contextual guidance may include:

- establish accepted responsibilities before a concrete example when the
  example could bias interpretation
- use the Resolver to select relevant documents rather than reading every
  document role
- consult supporting observations and design discussions when they help answer
  the current question
- resolve a language's declared guidance before using its Source as behavioral
  evidence
- use Raw only when curated documents do not provide enough context

## Boundary

The Documentation Resolver routes a current question to relevant documents.

Reading strategy guides how those documents may be interpreted in a particular
context. It is not a priority ranking among document roles or a mandatory
sequence through the Knowledge Pool.

## Question to observe

Can changes to guidance, document ordering, or entry points reduce the tendency
to mistake a concrete example for the concept itself, without changing
Author's Recipe?

Continue observing before treating this as a stable development practice.
