# Experiments

Experiments preserve questions, exploration, observations, rejected paths, and
candidate expressions from CTK's development.

This is a deliberately open area. An Experiment does not need to begin with a
complete structure or converge on a reusable conclusion.

## Questions that belong here

- What makes collaborative understanding emerge?
- Why do some informal conversations converge naturally?
- Is a stable destination sometimes more valuable than a complete document?
- Which observation should continue before it becomes a Note, Design Note, or
  accepted Knowledge?

## Freedom

Use the language and structure that best preserve the thought being explored.
English, Japanese, or a mixture of both are all acceptable.

An Experiment may be:

- a short hypothesis
- a long conversation or observation record
- a comparison of candidate models
- an active investigation
- a Reviewed record whose durable result has moved elsewhere

No shared heading set, status model, or writing style is required.

## Boundary

Experiments are not specifications and do not define accepted CTK
responsibilities.

When an observation becomes reusable without replaying its full exploration,
it may also become a [Project Knowledge Note](../note/README.md). Promotion does
not require deleting the Experiment; the path that produced the result may
remain valuable.

Return to [Project Knowledge](../README.md) when another lifecycle role is more
appropriate.
