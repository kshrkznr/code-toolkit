# Experiment: Unclassified Project Observations

## Status

Unreviewed source material moved from the former Project Knowledge Note index.

The statements are preserved together because their original context and
durable destinations are not yet clear. They should not be read as current CTK
specification or as one unified conclusion.

## Freeze evolution

Freeze was originally introduced as a simple import/export mechanism. Through
practical use, it evolved into the Draft Workbench, where people and AI could
review and refine Ingredients before they became part of the Cookbook.

## Implementation and concepts

> Implementation validates behavior. Usage validates concepts.

## Product direction

The product vision remained remarkably stable throughout development. Most
iterations refined the public concepts rather than changing the product's
overall direction.

## Review question

For each observation, determine whether it is already represented by current
Knowledge, remains useful as historical context, or has a distinct reusable
destination.
