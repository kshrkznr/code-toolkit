# Experiment: Can Past Thought Rejoin a Future Dialogue?

## Observation

After CTK began organizing conversations as Knowledge, the documents sometimes
felt less like records of the past and more like participants in the current
discussion.

Earlier framing and assumptions influenced later conversations even when the
documents did not prescribe a conclusion.

```text
Conversation
    ↓
Organize an observation as Knowledge
    ↓
Read it during a later conversation
    ↓
Develop a new concept or distinction
    ↓
Reflect the result into shared material
```

## Hypothesis

Documentation may do more than store information. It may act as a medium
through which past thought can re-enter a future dialogue.

This phrasing is deliberately tentative. A document does not literally become
a participant, and its influence depends on a current reader and context.

## What remains open

- Is this effect particular to conversations involving AI?
- Does the same effect already occur in human design documents, research notes,
  and RFCs?
- What information must be preserved for earlier thought to remain useful
  rather than becoming stale authority?
- When does retaining the path of thought help, and when does it merely add
  historical noise?
